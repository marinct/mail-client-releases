Mail that feels light.

Maily is a fast desktop mail client for Linux: your mail, calendars and contacts from Gmail, Outlook and any IMAP provider, in one calm window. It runs on your computer and talks only to your mail providers.

Download for Linux (.deb, AppImage)

Every accountGmail (Sign in with Google), Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail and your own domain, set up from your address.
Calendar and contactsYour Google and CalDAV/CardDAV calendars and address books, meeting invitations answered from the mail.
Private by designYour mail stays on your computer. No Maily servers, no tracking, no ads.
QuickKeyboard first, starts in a moment, uses a fraction of the memory of the big clients.

How your Google data is used

When you sign in with Google, Maily asks to read, send and organize your Gmail (over IMAP and SMTP), to see and edit your Google Calendar and to see and edit your Google contacts, so it can show and manage them for you in the app. That data is stored only on your computer and is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
